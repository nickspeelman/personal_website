Ed Zitron's AI crash / bubble paper trail

A chronological collection of Zitron repeatedly warning that the generative-AI boom is a bubble, predicting collapse, or describing scenarios in which AI companies and the wider tech market suffer a major crash.

A note on wording. “AI crash” is not always Zitron's exact formulation. Across these sources he variously predicts or discusses an AI bubble popping, a generative-AI collapse, the failure of OpenAI or another major AI company, and broader damage to tech stocks, jobs, data centers, and the tech industry. Some entries are direct predictions; others are explicitly conditional scenarios. The labels below try to preserve that distinction.
